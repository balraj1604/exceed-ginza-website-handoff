Property expertise. A broader purpose.

EXCEED REAL ESTATE is based in Dubai and works with property in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Our Japanese-speaking team supports clients with local knowledge and property expertise.

We aim to develop Ginza as a place for learning and exchange between Japan and the UAE. Business exchange, education and youth initiatives are future concepts; activities, partners and delivery arrangements will be announced as they are confirmed.

We believe experience and knowledge gained internationally can enrich Japanese businesses, communities and future generations.
