# Website story and implementation brief

## 核となるメッセージ

**世界を知ることから、未来は変わる。**

不動産を入り口に、日本とUAE、そして世界との接点をつくる。海外で得た経験・知識・人脈を、日本の企業、教育、地域社会、次の世代へと還元する。銀座を、物件を知る場所から、世界を知り、次の一歩を考える場所へ育てていく。

This is a synthesis of the original story, Shoya’s later feedback and the owner’s design direction—not an additional quotation from Shoya.

## Five-step cycle from Shoya’s diagram

1. **世界への入り口をつくる** — learn about overseas property, economics and culture through Ginza.
2. **世界を体験する** — consider visits, relocation, international education and business.
3. **世界で学び、成長する** — develop perspective, language, expertise and relationships.
4. **日本に価値を還元する** — connect experience to education, entrepreneurship, company internationalisation and communities.
5. **次世代の可能性を広げる** — make the next person’s first step more accessible.

## Public value: problems and intended contribution

- **Information and language barriers:** make overseas opportunities easier to understand through Japanese-language dialogue and professional connections.
- **Limited routes for SMEs to expand internationally:** use UAE networks as a potential starting point for learning, introductions and market exploration; expansion outcomes are not guaranteed.
- **Limited international exposure:** explore entrepreneur talks, guest conversations and online sharing as future opportunities, without claiming an active recurring programme.
- **Family and education concerns:** help families explore schooling, cultures and living conditions; communicate different choices without claiming one country’s education is universally superior.
- **Learning from technology adoption:** connect UAE examples of AI and mobility with Japan’s own work and priorities. Keep each example’s actual location, date and operating stage accurate.

## Current website direction

- Premium architecture-led presentation, soft image fades, no harsh horizontal separation.
- Armani opening image; copy aligned left, navigation and calls to action over the image.
- Glass-style metrics and showroom information panels; restrained count-up interaction.
- Four developer tabs: DAMAC community, Ellington tower, AMALI interior and BEYOND commercial space. Keep their distinct property types visible.
- Matched logo sizes, smaller lower captions, large high-quality photography.
- Ginza showroom supports seeing, comparing and discussing. The four featured developers lead the showcase; wider developer support and collaborations are part of the intended offering.
- Retain original property, area, team, testimonial and FAQ detail.
- Teruo’s message focuses on experience and opportunities built overseas; Shoya’s focuses on returning that experience to Japan.
- Opening: **19 November 2026**. Separate ceremony: **25 November 2026**, as confirmed by the owner. Do not imply government attendance or endorsement is confirmed.
- Elaborate cinematic scroll animation is deferred to a later phase.

Future plans must remain future-oriented. Do not convert ambitions into completed impact, invent statistics, describe the UAE as universally tax-free, or imply unconfirmed official partnerships.
